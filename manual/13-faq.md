# 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 🤔 General Questions

### Q: What is the SMEDI SME Database?
**A:** The SMEDI SME Database is a comprehensive digital platform designed to manage and track Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Malawi. It serves as a centralized repository for SME data, facilitating better policy-making, resource allocation, and support for the MSME sector.

### Q: Who can access the system?
**A:** Access is limited to authorized personnel including:
- Government officials and ministry staff
- Registered financial institutions and lenders
- Approved development partners and NGOs
- Authorized consultants and service providers
- System administrators and technical staff

### Q: Is there a cost to use the system?
**A:** No, the system is provided free of charge to authorized government users and approved stakeholders. However, access requires proper authorization and role assignment.

### Q: What browsers are supported?
**A:** The system supports modern web browsers including:
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari (Mac)

Ensure your browser is updated to the latest version for optimal performance.

### Q: Can I access the system on my mobile device?
**A:** Yes, the system is fully responsive and can be accessed on smartphones and tablets. However, some advanced features may work better on desktop computers with larger screens.

## 🔐 Login and Security

### Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
**A:** Follow these steps:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your email for a reset link (including spam folder)
5. Click the link and create a new password
6. Return to the login page with your new credentials

### Q: Why do I need such a complex password?
**A:** The system handles sensitive business and financial data, so strong passwords are essential for security. Password requirements include:
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least 1 capital letter
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 symbol
- No spaces

### Q: How long can I stay logged in?
**A:** For security reasons, you'll be automatically logged out after 30 minutes of inactivity. Save your work frequently to avoid losing data.

### Q: What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?
**A:** 2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring a code from your phone in addition to your password. It's required for high-privilege accounts like administrators and recommended for all users handling sensitive data.

### Q: Can I use the same password as other systems?
**A:** No, you should use a unique password for the SMEDI database. Never reuse passwords from other systems, as this creates security risks.

## 👥 Roles and Permissions

### Q: What's the difference between user roles?
**A:** User roles determine what you can access and do in the system:
- **Super Administrator**: Full system control
- **Administrator**: User management and system oversight
- **Librarian**: Full data management capabilities
- **Moderator**: Data review and limited editing
- **Member**: Basic data entry and viewing
- **Guest**: Read-only access to public information

### Q: Why can't I see certain menu items or data?
**A:** Your access is determined by your user role and assigned scope. If you need access to additional features or data, contact your supervisor or system administrator to request appropriate permissions.

### Q: How do I request a role change?
**A:** Contact your supervisor or system administrator with:
- Your current role and desired role
- Business justification for the change
- Approval from your department head
- Any relevant training certifications

### Q: Can I have multiple roles?
**A:** No, each user is assigned one primary role. However, roles are hierarchical, so higher roles include permissions from lower roles.

## 📊 Data Entry and Management

### Q: How do I add a new SME record?
**A:** To add a new SME:
1. Navigate to SME Management → Add New SME
2. Fill in all required fields (marked with *)
3. Upload any required documents
4. Review your entry for accuracy
5. Click "Save" to create the record

### Q: What information is required for SME registration?
**A:** Required information includes:
- Business name and registration number
- Business type and industry category
- Contact information (address, phone, email)
- Owner/manager details
- Basic financial information (if applicable)
- Supporting documents (registration certificates, etc.)

### Q: Can I edit records after they're created?
**A:** Yes, if you have appropriate permissions. Your ability to edit depends on:
- Your user role
- Whether you created the record
- The approval status of the record
- Any workflow restrictions

### Q: How do I upload documents?
**A:** To upload documents:
1. Look for the "Upload" or "Attach Files" button
2. Click and select files from your computer
3. Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG
4. Maximum file size: 10MB per file
5. Add descriptions for each document

### Q: What if I make a mistake in data entry?
**A:** If you notice an error:
- **Before saving**: Simply correct the field and save
- **After saving**: Edit the record if you have permission, or contact your supervisor
- **Critical errors**: Contact your administrator immediately

## 🔍 Search and Reports

### Q: How do I search for specific SME records?
**A:** Use the search features:
- **Global Search**: Use the search bar at the top of any page
- **Advanced Search**: Click the gear icon next to the search bar
- **Filters**: Use the filter panel on list pages
- **Quick Filters**: Use predefined filter buttons

### Q: Why don't my search results show all the data I expect?
**A:** Search results are filtered based on:
- Your user permissions and scope
- Geographic or organizational restrictions
- Data sensitivity levels
- Active filters that may be hiding results

### Q: How do I generate reports?
**A:** To create reports:
1. Go to Reports & Analytics
2. Choose from standard reports or create custom reports
3. Set your parameters (date range, filters, etc.)
4. Preview the report
5. Generate and download in your preferred format

### Q: What report formats are available?
**A:** Reports can be exported in:
- PDF (for sharing and printing)
- Excel (for further analysis)
- CSV (for data processing)
- Word (for formatted documents)

### Q: Can I schedule automatic reports?
**A:** Currently, automatic report scheduling is available only to administrators. Contact your administrator if you need regular automated reports.

## 💻 Technical Issues

### Q: The system is running slowly. What can I do?
**A:** Try these solutions:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs
3. Disable browser extensions temporarily
4. Check your internet connection speed
5. Try using a different browser
6. Restart your computer if necessary

### Q: I'm getting error messages. What should I do?
**A:** For error messages:
1. Take a screenshot of the error
2. Note what you were doing when it occurred
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Clear your browser cache
5. If the error persists, contact technical support

### Q: Can I work offline?
**A:** Limited offline functionality is available:
- View previously loaded data
- Work on forms that were already open
- Access cached reports
However, you need an internet connection to save data or load new information.

### Q: What if I lose my internet connection while working?
**A:** The system automatically saves drafts for most forms. When your connection is restored:
1. Refresh the page
2. Check for any draft notifications
3. Review and complete any unsaved work
4. Save your work manually to ensure it's preserved

## 📱 Mobile and Accessibility

### Q: Does the system work on tablets and smartphones?
**A:** Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on mobile devices. Some features may be easier to use on larger screens, but all core functionality is available on mobile.

### Q: Are there accessibility features for users with disabilities?
**A:** Yes, the system includes:
- Screen reader compatibility
- Keyboard navigation support
- High contrast mode
- Adjustable font sizes
- Focus indicators
- Alt text for images

### Q: Can I change the interface language?
**A:** Currently, the system supports English and Chichewa. You can change the language in your profile settings.

## 🔧 Account Management

### Q: How do I update my profile information?
**A:** To update your profile:
1. Click your name in the top right corner
2. Select "Profile Settings"
3. Edit the information you want to change
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Q: Can I change my email address?
**A:** Email address changes must be requested through your system administrator, as your email is used for system authentication and notifications.

### Q: How do I receive notifications about system updates?
**A:** Notifications are sent through:
- In-system notification panel
- Email notifications (check your settings)
- System announcements on the dashboard

### Q: What happens if I leave my position?
**A:** When leaving your position:
1. Complete any pending work
2. Transfer responsibilities to colleagues
3. Inform your supervisor about ongoing projects
4. Your account will be deactivated by the administrator
